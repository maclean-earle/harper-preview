One price per person. One bill a month.

Harper is health care for small teams that don't have an insurance plan. You pay one flat price for each employee, every month. Your crew gets credits to spend at local clinics.

  • No plan to shop for. There are no plan options to compare.
  • No copays or deductibles. Your team uses credits instead.
  • No claims. The clinic gets paid by us, not by you.
  • One place to run it. Your employer portal works on the web.
