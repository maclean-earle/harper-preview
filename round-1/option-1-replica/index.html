How it works

From one monthly price to a paid visit, in three steps.

  1. Add your team

    Tell us how many people work for you. We bill your business on the 1st, and every employee gets credits.

  2. Book and get a PIN

    Your employee picks a local provider, books a visit, and gets a new PIN for that visit.

  3. The provider gets paid

    The provider enters the PIN to redeem a credit. Harper pays them on the 15th of the next month.
